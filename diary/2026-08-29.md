## Key Decisions & Architecture
- Enforced the invariant that contradictions are never resolved by timestamp recency alone; provenance and confidence always take precedence.
- Excluded superseded and archived memories from normal agent retrieval to prevent cognitive confusion while preserving historical recall.
- Integrated dual soft/hard deletion: soft deletion preserves audit history, whereas hard deletion purges both relational and vector stores.
- Scheduled automatic importance decay for aging unverified records to prioritize active, high-confidence memory retention.
- Preserved complete relational links and superseded_by_id references to support historical audit investigations.
