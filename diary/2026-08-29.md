## Bugs I Found & Fixed
- **Grounding Hybrid Score Threshold**: `AIUniverseAdapter.ground_model_reasoning` used a high similarity threshold (`min_score=0.4`) incompatible with Reciprocal Rank Fusion score scales ($0.01 - 0.15$).
  - *Fix*: Adjusted `min_score=0.0` for RRF retrieval in `adapters/ai_universe/adapter.py`.
- **Adapter Client Protocol Mapping**: Adapters required seamless support for both standalone `httpx.Client` instances and FastAPI `TestClient` objects.
  - *Fix*: Implemented dynamic client method resolution in `BaseAgentAdapter._dispatch_request`.
- **Identity Grant Agent Resolution**: `IdentityService.grant_access` required UUID `agent_id` when callers provided `agent_name`.
  - *Fix*: Updated `IdentityService.grant_access` to accept `agent_name` or `agent_id` and resolve dynamically.
