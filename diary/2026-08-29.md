## Bugs I Found & Fixed
- **Summary Prefix Token Inflation**: Static prefix strings in synthetic summaries caused boundary token counts to exceed target budgets by 1 token.
  - *Fix*: Bounded full text length including prefixes strictly within `target_tokens * CHARS_PER_TOKEN`.
- **Lexical Distracter Bias**: Keyword-heavy non-solutions received inflated initial search scores over conceptual answers.
  - *Fix*: Integrated neural cross-encoder semantic interaction scoring blending $lpha \cdot Score_{ce} + (1-lpha) \cdot Score_{coarse}$.
- **Remote Model Download Timeout**: HuggingFace remote weight downloads in offline or throttled environments caused execution delay.
  - *Fix*: Implemented `NeuralCrossEncoderEngine` with deterministic semantic interaction fallback and configurable remote loading.
